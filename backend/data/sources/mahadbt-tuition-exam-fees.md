# Tuition Fees and Examination Fees to VJNT, SBC and OBC Students (MahaDBT)

## Overview
The "Payment of Tuition Fees and Examination Fees to VJNT, SBC and OBC Students" is a prominent flagship state scholarship scheme run by the Government of Maharashtra under the OBC, Bahujan Welfare Department (इतर मागास बहुजन कल्याण विभाग) on the official MahaDBT portal (https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in). The scheme aims to encourage higher education among students belonging to Vimukta Jatis (VJ), Nomadic Tribes (NT), Special Backward Classes (SBC), and Other Backward Classes (OBC) by reimbursing their mandatory tuition fees and examination fees in recognized post-matric professional and non-professional courses across Maharashtra.

## Key Benefits and Financial Assistance
Under this MahaDBT scheme, eligible students receive substantial fee relief directly credited to their colleges or verified bank accounts:
1. For Government and Government-Aided Institutions: 100% of the tuition fees and exam fees are reimbursed by the Government of Maharashtra.
2. For Unaided / Private Non-Aided Institutions: 50% to 100% reimbursement of tuition fees and exam fees is provided as approved by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA) / Shikshan Shulka Samiti.
3. Covers all recognized diploma, undergraduate degree, postgraduate, and professional degree courses (such as Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, Architecture, Law, Management, Agriculture, and Arts/Science/Commerce) admitted through Centralized Admission Process (CAP).

## Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for Tuition and Examination Fees Reimbursement on MahaDBT:
1. Category Requirement: The applicant must belong to VJNT (VJ / NT-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D), SBC (Special Backward Class), or OBC (Other Backward Class) recognized in Maharashtra state.
2. Domicile: The candidate must be a resident and holder of a Domicile Certificate of Maharashtra state.
3. Income Limit: Total annual family income from all sources must not exceed Rs 8,00,000 (Eight Lakhs) per annum. An official income certificate issued by a competent revenue authority (Tahsildar or Sub-Divisional Officer) is mandatory.
4. Admission Criteria: The candidate must be admitted through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) for professional courses or through regular recognized merit admission in government/aided/unaided institutions.
5. Minimum Passing Standard: The student must have passed the previous year examination and maintain satisfactory attendance.

## Required Documents
Applicants must upload the following clear scanned documents on the MahaDBT portal:
1. Domicile Certificate of Maharashtra State
2. Caste Certificate (VJNT / SBC / OBC issued by competent authority in Maharashtra)
3. Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate valid for the current academic year
4. Income Certificate issued by Tahsildar (annual family income up to Rs 8,00,000)
5. SSC (10th) and HSC (12th) Marksheet or previous year qualifying examination mark sheet
6. College Admission Fee Receipt and CAP Allotment Letter
7. Aadhaar Seeded Bank Account Passbook copy

## How to Apply on MahaDBT Portal
Citizens and students must apply strictly online through the MahaDBT portal:
1. Visit the official Maharashtra Direct Benefit Transfer portal at https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
2. Register as a new applicant or login with existing credentials under "Post Matric Scholarship".
3. Navigate to Department: "OBC, SEBC, VJNT & SBC Welfare Department" (इतर मागास बहुजन कल्याण विभाग).
4. Select the scheme: "Payment of Tuition Fees and Examination Fees to VJNT / SBC / OBC Students".
5. Fill educational details, upload verified certificates, and submit the application for college verification.
