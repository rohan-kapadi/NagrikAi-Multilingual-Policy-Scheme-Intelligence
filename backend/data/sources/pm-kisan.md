# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) Guidelines

## 1. Scheme Overview
The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India to provide income support to all landholding farmer families across the country.

## 2. Benefits and Financial Assistance
Under the scheme, financial assistance of Rs 6,000 per year is provided to eligible beneficiary farmer families. The benefit is transferred directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries in three equal installments of Rs 2,000 each every four months.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. The beneficiary family must own cultivable agricultural land as per the land records of the respective State/UT.
2. The primary occupation must be agriculture / farming.
3. Both small and marginal farmers, as well as larger farmers holding cultivable land, are covered subject to statutory exclusions.

## 4. Ineligibility and Exclusions
The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status are excluded from the scheme:
- All institutional landholders.
- Farmer families in which one or more of its members hold or held constitutional posts.
- Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries and PSUs.
- All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year or have extraordinary annual income exceeding the permissible ceilings.

## 5. Required Documents and Application Procedure
To avail the benefit, farmers must furnish:
- Aadhaar number linked with bank account.
- Land holding documentation (7/12 extract, Jamabandi, or mutation register).
- Active bank account details (IFSC code and account number).
Eligible farmers can enroll themselves via the official PM-KISAN portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) under the 'Farmers Corner' or visit local Common Service Centres (CSC).
