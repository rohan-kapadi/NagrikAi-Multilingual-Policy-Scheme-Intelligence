# Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme Guidelines

## 1. Scheme Objective
The Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme aimed at providing financial assistance to students belonging to low-income families to enable them to complete post-matriculation or post-secondary education.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility Conditions
1. The applicant must be a regular student enrolled in a recognized secondary school, junior college, degree college, or university pursuing post-matric courses (Class 11, Class 12, Diploma, Graduation, or Post-Graduation).
2. The total annual family income of the parents/guardians from all sources must not exceed Rs 2,50,000 per annum.
3. The candidate must have successfully passed Class 10 or the previous qualifying examination.

## 3. Scholarship Value and Benefits
The scholarship coverage includes:
- Reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees (tuition fees, library charges, examination fees) as fixed by the State Fee Regulatory Committee.
- Maintenance allowance payable per month according to day scholar or hosteller status for the academic duration.

## 4. Mandatory Documentation
Applicants must upload the following verified documents:
- Attested copy of Class 10 mark sheet and previous academic certificate.
- Income Certificate issued by a competent Revenue Authority (Tahsildar or Sub-Divisional Magistrate) not older than one financial year.
- Valid admission confirmation fee receipt of the current academic year.
- Aadhaar seeded bank account passbook.

## 5. How to Apply
Applications must be submitted digitally via the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at https://scholarships.gov.in. Hard copies need not be submitted to the Ministry; verification is conducted digitally by the educational institution and state nodal officer.
